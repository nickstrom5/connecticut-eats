# Naming, icon and palette (Nick's calls, 2026-10-05)

## Name
**Connecticut Eats: Restaurants** (App Store, 29), subtitle **Apizza & Lobster Roll Guide** (27), Home Screen **CT Eats**.
Matches "Wisconsin Eats: Restaurants" and the eatsranked.com hub (connecticut.eatsranked.com).

Checked 2026-10-05:
- App Store search (iTunes Search API) for "connecticut eats", "ct eats", "nutmeg eats", "nutmeg plates", "connecticut restaurants":
  no app with these names. Connecticut queries are led by the CT Insider news app, Yelp, The Infatuation and Resy.
- Apple's search autocomplete has no suggestions for "connecticut res", "ct eats", "nutmeg" (low existing traffic).
- Domains: connecticuteats.com is a parked GoDaddy lander (registered 2011); nutmegeats.com and nutmegplates.com unregistered;
  cteats.com registered April 2026 (no site). The site lives at connecticut.eatsranked.com either way.
- One web search found no app, show or trademark named "Connecticut Eats" or "Nutmeg Eats" (only Nutmeg Farms CT's convenience-store
  mark and the Nutmeg Restaurant in East Windsor).
- Avoided: "CT Bites" (CTbites.com), state brand lines ("Make It Here", "Still Revolutionary").

Runners-up shown to Nick: Nutmeg Eats: CT Restaurants; CT Restaurants: Ranked ("Connecticut Restaurants: Ranked" is 31, over 30);
Nutmeg Plates: CT Restaurants.

## Palette
UConn-inspired navy #000E2F, white, gray #7C878E (rules only; 3.7:1 on white), tomato #B3261E fill with white text (6.4:1). No Husky
marks. Header element: a Long Island Sound shoreline wave. No flag, seal or grapevine arms (CGS §3-106a needs the Secretary of the
State's approval).
