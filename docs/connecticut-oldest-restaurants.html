How the years were checked

Each year is when the place opened at the address where it is now, checked against the restaurant's own history page or local news. A business that moved counts from its move, so an older name can sit lower on this list. A year is a fact about the address, not a rating, and the list only covers places we checked by hand; the rest of the app's 9,358 restaurants have no checked year.

How the list was built: Every place was checked in business in October 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source: its own website or menu, a dated post, local news, or a tourism listing, with the address checked too. Seasonal places list their season, so check it before you drive. Places with no current source were left out. Nothing here comes from review sites, and the order is by years at the same address, never by anyone's rating. Hours and menus change, so check before you go.

In the Connecticut Eats app and the web search, the Oldest Places guide has the same list. For more on what these places serve, see the apizza, lobster roll, hot dog and burger, and diner and dairy bar guides.
