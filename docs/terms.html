Terms of use

The plain-language terms for the Connecticut Eats app and this website. Last updated Oct 7, 2026.

What the app is

Connecticut Eats is a free guide to restaurants in Connecticut. It is provided as is, for personal use, without charge and without warranties of any kind.

Check before you go

Restaurants open, close, and change their hours and menus, and shacks and dairy bars close for the season. Dishes, seasons, and years at an address are what each place or a named source said when we checked in October 2026. We work to keep the lists right, but we can't promise that any listing is current or complete. Call the restaurant before you make the trip.

Health ratings

Connecticut has no statewide restaurant inspection results or grades, and Connecticut Eats doesn't grade restaurants. For the 10 towns of the Farmington Valley Health District (Avon, Barkhamsted, Canton, Colebrook, East Granby, Farmington, Granby, Hartland, New Hartford, and Simsbury), the app and the web search show the district's own official rating (A Excellent, B Good, C Fair, or U Unsatisfactory) with its date, as the district published it. A rating describes one inspection on that day. For the official record, see the Farmington Valley Health District.

Casino restaurants

Restaurants inside Foxwoods Resort Casino and Mohegan Sun are on Mashantucket Pequot and Mohegan tribal land, and the app labels them that way.
