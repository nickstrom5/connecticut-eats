Connecticut diners & dairy bars

11 long-running diners and 34 dairy bars, farm creameries, and ice cream stands, each checked in business in October 2026 on its own website, menu, or recent local news.

Breakfast, then a cone

Diners on this list are the counter-and-booth kind, open for breakfast and lunch and often longer. Dairy bars range from farm stands that make ice cream from their own cows' milk to roadside walk-up windows. 10 of the 34 dairy bars are seasonal, so check the season listed with each.

113 other places have "Diner" in their name. They're in the app's full directory, but they're not on this list until we check them. The longest at the same address here: Heibeck's Stand in Georgetown (Wilton), there since 1931; Shady Glen in Manchester, there since 1948; Olympia Diner in Newington, there since 1954; Gooseboro Drive-In in Bantam (Litchfield), there since 1961. See Connecticut's oldest restaurants for all 55 with a year at the same address.

How the list was built: Every place was checked in business in October 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source: its own website or menu, a dated post, local news, or a tourism listing, with the address checked too. Seasonal places list their season, so check it before you drive. Places with no current source were left out. Nothing here comes from review sites, and the order is by region and town, never by anyone's rating. Hours and menus change, so check before you go.

In the Connecticut Eats app, diners and dairy bars are separate lists that sort by distance from you. Hungry first? See the hot dog and burger icons.
