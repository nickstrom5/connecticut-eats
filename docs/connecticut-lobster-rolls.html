58 places along the shore and inland for a hot buttered lobster roll, fried clams or both, each confirmed open in October 2026. 19 of them are seasonal, so check the season listed with each before you drive.

Hot, with butter

A Connecticut lobster roll is served warm: the meat is tossed in melted butter and piled into a toasted roll. The cold kind, dressed with mayonnaise, is the Maine style, and many places here serve both. Clam shacks add fried clams, clam strips and chowder, and many of them close for the winter.

The 58 on this list

56 serve a lobster roll and 28 are clam shacks, many both. 19 are seasonal; 15 post their season, which is listed with each place. The longest at the same address: Heibeck's Stand in Georgetown (Wilton), there since 1931; Abbott's Lobster In the Rough in Noank (Groton), there since 1947; Johnny Ad's in Old Saybrook, there since 1957; Seven Seas Restaurant in Milford, there since 1965.

How the list was built: Every place was checked open in October 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source: its own website or menu, a dated post, local news or a tourism listing, with the address checked too. Places with no current source were left out. Nothing here comes from review sites, and the order is by region and town, never by anyone's rating. Hours and menus change, so check before you go.

In the Connecticut Eats app, the list sorts by distance from you, and each place opens Apple Maps' own card with live hours. Inland? See the hot dog and burger icons and the dairy bars.
