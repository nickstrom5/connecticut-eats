Steamed, stood at, driven to

The steamed cheeseburger belongs to central Connecticut: the burger and a block of cheddar are steamed in small metal trays, and the melted cheese is poured over the top. Hot dog stands and drive-ins with a long history fill out the rest of this list.

The 21 on this list

14 are hot dog places and 8 are burger places, 3 of them serving steamed cheeseburgers (in Meriden and Wallingford). The longest at the same address: Blackie's Hot Dog Stand in Cheshire, there since 1928; Heibeck's Stand in Georgetown (Wilton), there since 1931; Rawley's Drive-in in Fairfield, there since 1947; Shady Glen Restaurant and Ice Cream Parlor in Manchester, there since 1948.

How the list was built: Every place was checked open in October 2026 against a 2025 or 2026 source: its own website or menu, a dated post, local news or a tourism listing, with the address checked too. Places with no current source were left out. Nothing here comes from review sites, and the order is by region and town, never by anyone's rating. Hours and menus change, so check before you go.

In the Connecticut Eats app, the list sorts by distance from you. For dessert after, see the dairy bars and diners.
